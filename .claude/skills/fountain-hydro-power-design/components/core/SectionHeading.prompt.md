Opens a page or slide section with the brand's eyebrow + heading + 3px teal rule pattern.

```jsx
<SectionHeading eyebrow="Mantenimiento electromecánico" description="Resumen de la semana 24.">
  Disponibilidad y trabajos ejecutados
</SectionHeading>
```

Eyebrows are all caps and tracked; headings stay sentence case (Spanish orthography).
