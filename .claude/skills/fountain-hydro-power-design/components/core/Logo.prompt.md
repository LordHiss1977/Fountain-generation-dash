Places an official FHPC logo lockup; never re-typeset or redraw the mark by hand.

```jsx
<Logo variant="primary" background="light" width={320} basePath="../../assets/logos" />
```

Variants: `primary` (full lockup with tagline), `secondary` ("FOUNTAIN" only, for footers and tight widths), `mark` (drop alone, never below 24px). Backgrounds: `light`, `dark`, `negative`. Keep one drop-width of clear space around it.
