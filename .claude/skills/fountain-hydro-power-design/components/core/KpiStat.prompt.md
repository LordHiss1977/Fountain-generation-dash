Displays one operational number — availability, generation, hours — as the visual anchor of a card or slide.

```jsx
<KpiStat label="Disponibilidad" value="98,4" unit="%" delta="+1,2 pp" deltaTone="up" caption="vs. semana 23" />
```

Figures are Bahnschrift, tabular-lining, Spanish decimal comma. Label is all caps and tracked above the number.
